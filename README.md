# KapoleiBioForge CAD Portfolio

**Michael Lozada-Longog | BME '29 | Hawaii Pacific University | Kapolei, Hawaii**

Medical device component designs built in Fusion 360 with static stress FEA analysis. Each project documents the design process, material selection, loading conditions, and structural validation results.

---

## Project 1 — IV Drip Holder Clamp

Bed rail IV pole clamp designed for hospital use. C-channel body with screw boss, pole collar, grip pad, and chamfered rail entry.

**FEA Results — Static Stress under 50N load**
- Max Von Mises Stress: 0.966 MPa
- Safety Factor: Above 8.0 across all regions
- Max Displacement: 4.678E-05 inches
- Result: Very Strong — well below ABS yield strength of 40 MPa

---

## Project 2 — Knee Brace Hinge

Two-arm revolute hinge assembly with 0-120 degree range of motion matching clinical knee flexion requirements.

**FEA Results — Static Stress under 50N load**
- Max Von Mises Stress: 0.446 MPa
- Safety Factor: 15.00 minimum
- Max Displacement: 0.000206 inches
- Result: Very Strong — stress concentration confirmed at pivot bend per engineering theory

---

## Project 3 — Spinal Brace Clip

C-channel ABS attachment clip with M3 screw mounting, locking tab retention, and chamfered rail entry.

**FEA Results — Static Stress under 100N load**
- Max Von Mises Stress: 8.193 MPa
- Safety Factor: 2.441 minimum
- Max Displacement: 0.002 inches
- Result: Exceeds 2.0 medical hardware safety factor minimum
